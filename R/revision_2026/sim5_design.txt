Simulation 5 design record
Written on 20 October 2026 before running the new simulation

Aim
Assess recovery of between-group differences in structural symptom couplings
when context differs between groups, its measurement is imperfect, and true
couplings may also differ. This is a parameter recovery study, not an evaluation
of a new estimator or a test of causal identification from observational data.

Generating model
Use the nine symptoms, baseline activation parameters, ten nonzero couplings,
and contextual sensitivities already specified in 00_parameters_uncentered01.R.
Draw independent symptom profiles exactly from all 512 states of the
conditional equilibrium Ising distribution. The context is constant for each
person. There is no simulated temporal feedback in this study.

Six scenarios
1 Neither difference. Both groups have P distributed N(0, 0.6^2) and equal J.
2 Context only. Group 0 has P distributed N(-0.5, 0.35^2), group 1
  N(0.5, 1^2), and both have equal J.
3 Coupling only. Both groups have P distributed N(0, 0.6^2). Group 1 has
  three couplings increased by 0.30.
4 Both differences. Context differs as in scenario 2 and couplings as in 3.
5 Balanced coupling changes. Context differs as in scenario 2. The
  anhedonia-depressed coupling increases by 0.30 and the sleep-energy
  coupling decreases by 0.30, leaving the signed sum unchanged.
6 Context mean only. Group 0 has P distributed N(-0.5, 0.6^2), group 1
  N(0.5, 0.6^2), and couplings are equal.

The three increasing couplings are anhedonia-depressed, sleep-energy, and
energy-concentration. These were chosen before execution and are not fitted
to HELIUS. The baseline topology is fixed across scenarios.

Sample sizes and repetitions
500 and 2000 independent people per group. 500 independent replicates per
scenario and sample size, totaling 6000 datasets with two groups each.
All methods use the same profiles within a replicate. Replicate seeds depend
only on scenario, sample size, and replicate number. The number of replicates
is fixed before execution, not increased until a desired conclusion appears.
Monte Carlo standard errors quantify remaining simulation uncertainty.

Methods
Unpenalized nodewise logistic regression with the two directed coefficients
for each symptom pair averaged. Fit each group separately, allowing all 36
couplings to differ in every method.
Compare omission of context, inclusion of true P, and inclusion of a noisy
measurement W at within-group reliability 0.8, 0.5, or 0.2.
W = P + sigma_g sqrt((1-r)/r) epsilon, with epsilon standard normal and
independent of context and symptoms. sigma_g is the generating standard
deviation of P within group g. The same epsilon is reused across reliability
levels. Reliability is controlled by design, not estimated from each sample.
There is no correction for measurement error in the fitted model.

Estimands and performance
The primary estimands are the 36 structural differences J_1 - J_0.
Report root mean squared error across these differences for each replicate,
the signed-sum error, mean absolute error on unchanged pairs, and recovery
of changed pairs. For changed pairs, orient estimates by the sign of the true
change before averaging, with target 0.30.
Also retain every edge estimate to report edge-specific Monte Carlo bias
and root mean squared error. Average performance and Monte Carlo standard
errors are computed across replicates. Paired differences compare methods
on the same simulated data. No hypothesis-test rejection rate is interpreted
as a causal false-positive rate.

Numerical checks and failures
Validate the optimized nodewise fitter against R glm and exact state
probabilities against their nodewise logistic conditionals. Check convergence,
finite estimates, and score residuals. Keep a record of every failure and
report denominators. Never replace failed estimates by zero.

Scope
The design isolates measurement error under a single, linear, exogenous
contextual cause. It does not address omitted additional causes, endogenous
covariates, nonlinearity, item measurement noninvariance, or data-dependent
group formation. It does not establish performance of exact-likelihood model
selection, the Network Comparison Test, or causal interaction tests.
The design record is local and was not publicly preregistered.

Numerical amendment after the first full execution
The initial convergence criterion accepted some large iterates in separated
logistic regressions. Before interpreting results, a zero-cell separation
screen was added. Remaining fits with any coefficient above 8 in absolute
value receive a linear-programming separation check. A large coefficient alone
is not a reason for exclusion. Known separated and nonseparated fixtures
validate the checks. The original scenario grid, seeds, sample sizes, and
replication count remain unchanged. All methods are summarized on the same
set of successful datasets within a scenario and sample size. Failure counts
are reported separately, so recovery summaries are conditional on estimability.
